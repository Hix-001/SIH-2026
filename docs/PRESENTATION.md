# SIH 2026 Jury Presentation & Pitch Script

**Project Title:** Citizen Legal Triage & Automated Rights Navigator (NyayaSetu)  
**Theme:** Smart Automation  
**Target Audience:** 1.4 Billion Indian Citizens, Legal Aid Defense Counsel (NALSA), State Legal Services Authorities.

---

## 3-Minute Pitch Script

### 1. The Hook (0:00 - 0:45)
> "Judges, consider this: An ordinary citizen in Bengaluru vacates their flat. The landlord holds their ₹80,000 security deposit for 60 days. Or a small shopkeeper falls victim to a ₹40,000 UPI electricity scam.
> 
> What do they do today? They search online and are drowned in obsolete IPC sections, complex legal jargon, and exorbitant legal consultation fees. Under India's new criminal codes—the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS 2023)—citizens are even more lost."

### 2. The Solution (0:45 - 1:45)
> "Introducing **NyayaSetu**: India's premier AI-powered Citizen Legal Triage & Automated Rights Navigator.
> 
> With NyayaSetu:
> 1. **Any citizen can speak or type in 22 Indic languages** (powered by Bhashini AI).
> 2. **Zero-Knowledge PII Scrubbing**: Before processing, sensitive personal data—Aadhaar, PAN, phone numbers, and bank accounts—are stripped directly in the client's browser.
> 3. **Smart Statutory Mapping**: The system instantly maps the dispute to exact **BNS 2023** sections (e.g. BNS 303 for deposit withholding, IT Act 66D for UPI fraud), showing old IPC comparisons, penalties, and cognizable classifications.
> 4. **Automated Legal Notice Generation**: In one click, it synthesizes an official 15-day statutory legal notice formatted to Indian court standards, ready to download as a signed PDF or print for registered speed post."

### 3. Technical Highlights (1:45 - 2:30)
> "- **Client-Side Privacy Engine**: Built on DPDP Act 2023 principles.
> - **Web Speech API**: Real-time voice waveform and speech-to-text.
> - **Complete Statutory Knowledge Base**: BNS 2023, IT Act 2000, CPA 2019, Section 138 NI Act, RERA.
> - **Automated PDF Notice & Dossier Generator**: One-click professional exports for police complaints or advocate handoff."

### 4. The Impact (2:30 - 3:00)
> "NyayaSetu fulfills the constitutional promise of **Article 39A—Equal Justice and Free Legal Aid**—turning confusion into actionable legal empowerment."

## Key Demonstration Flows for Evaluators

1. **Preset Test 1 (Landlord Dispute):**
   - Click *Landlord Withholding Deposit* preset.
   - Observe real-time PII masking of Aadhaar and Phone.
   - Run triage -> see BNS Section 303 (Criminal Breach of Trust) vs IPC 405/406.
   - Switch to *Legal Notice Drafter* -> Click *Download PDF* to see the formal notice.

2. **Preset Test 2 (Online UPI Scam):**
   - Click *Fake Electricity Bill UPI Scam* preset.
   - Notice the *CRITICAL PRIORITY* alert and 1930 Cyber Helpline Golden Hour notice.
   - See Section 66D of IT Act mapping.

3. **Multilingual Test:**
   - Toggle language selector to **हिन्दी (Hindi)** or **தமிழ் (Tamil)** to see native legal navigation.
